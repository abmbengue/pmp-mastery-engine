## 2. PEOPLE — 8 TASKS
Source: `PMI_ECO_2026` · Confidence: HIGH · ECO FR pages 8–9.

English titles: DERIVED paraphrases of French primary wording.

### Task 1 — PEOPLE-T01 / PE-01 — Develop a common vision
FR primary: Développer une vision commune.
Shared vision, promotion, keeping vision current, root-cause analysis of misunderstanding.

### Task 2 — PEOPLE-T02 / PE-02 — Manage conflicts
FR primary: Gérer les conflits.
Identify source, analyze context, implement resolution, communicate principles, establish/maintain ground rules.

### Task 3 — PEOPLE-T03 / PE-03 — Lead the project team
FR primary: Diriger l'équipe de projet.
Set expectations, empower, solve problems, represent team voice, support varied perspectives, choose leadership style, clarify roles.

### Task 4 — PEOPLE-T04 / PE-04 — Engage stakeholders
FR primary: Impliquer les parties prenantes.
Identify/analyze stakeholders, tailor communication, execute engagement, align needs/expectations/objectives, build trust and influence.

**Critical distinction:** T4 = **ENGAGEMENT** (participation, alignment, relationship). Related to but **not the same as** T8 communication planning.

### Task 5 — PEOPLE-T05 / PE-05 — Align stakeholder expectations
FR primary: Aligner les attentes des parties prenantes.
Categorize stakeholders, identify expectations, facilitate alignment discussions, mentoring opportunities.

### Task 6 — PEOPLE-T06 / PE-06 — Manage stakeholder expectations
FR primary: Gérer les attentes des parties prenantes.
Identify customer expectations, maintain outcome alignment, monitor satisfaction/expectations and respond.

### Task 7 — PEOPLE-T07 / PE-07 — Ensure knowledge transfer
FR primary: Assurer le transfert des connaissances.
Source: `PMI_ECO_2026` · Page 9 · Confidence: HIGH.

Enablers (paraphrased from ECO catalyseurs):
- identify knowledge essential to the project
- acquire knowledge
- foster an environment that supports knowledge transfer

Do **not** demote T7 to a non-ECO theme because knowledge also appears in PMBOK/closure contexts.

### Task 8 — PEOPLE-T08 / PE-08 — Plan and manage communication
FR primary: Planifier et gérer la communication.
Source: `PMI_ECO_2026` · Page 9 · Confidence: HIGH.

Enablers (paraphrased from ECO catalyseurs):
- establish a communication strategy
- promote transparency and collaboration
- establish a feedback loop
- understand reporting requirements
- create reports aligned with sponsor and stakeholder expectations
- support reporting and governance processes

**Critical distinction:** T8 = **COMMUNICATION** planning/management as a **separate ECO task**. Communication may support engagement (T4), but T8 is not a sub-concept of T4.

Mastery pair: communication ≠ engagement (`mc-communication-engagement`).

## 3. PROCESS — 10 TASKS
Source: `PMI_ECO_2026` · Confidence: HIGH · ECO FR pages 10–11.

### Task 1 — PROCESS-T01 / PR-01 — Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery
Assess needs/complexity, choose predictive/adaptive/hybrid approach, identify critical information, execution strategy, integrated plan, effort/resources, dependencies/gaps/value, data-informed decisions.

### Task 2 — PROCESS-T02 / PR-02 — Develop and manage project scope
Define scope, obtain stakeholder agreement, break down scope.

### Task 3 — PROCESS-T03 / PR-03 — Help ensure value-based delivery
Identify value, prioritize using value/feedback, incremental delivery, monitor business value, benefits measurement, delivery options.

### Task 4 — PROCESS-T04 / PR-04 — Plan and manage resources
Define/plan resources; manage and optimize needs and availability.

### Task 5 — PROCESS-T05 / PR-05 — Plan and manage procurement
Plan procurement, execute procurement management, select contract types, evaluate vendors, verify agreement objectives, negotiate, manage suppliers/contracts, procurement strategy, delivery solution.

### Task 6 — PROCESS-T06 / PR-06 — Plan and manage finance
Financial needs, contingency/risk allocations, spend tracking, reporting, future finance challenges, financial variations/governance, reserves.

### Task 7 — PROCESS-T07 / PR-07 — Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables
Quality requirements, processes/tools, quality plan, regulatory compliance, cost of quality/sustainability, ongoing reviews, continuous improvement.

### Task 8 — PROCESS-T08 / PR-08 — Plan and manage schedule
Schedule by development approach, coordination, estimates, milestones/dependencies/story points, benchmarks/history, schedule creation/baseline, management, variance analysis.

### Task 9 — PROCESS-T09 / PR-09 — Evaluate project status
Metrics, analysis/reconciliation, artifact tailoring and management, accessibility, progress assessment, metric updates, status communication.

### Task 10 — PROCESS-T10 / PR-10 — Manage project closure
Stakeholder approval, closure criteria, transition readiness, lessons learned/retrospectives, procurement/financial/resource closeout.

## 5. PMBOK 8 — SEVEN PERFORMANCE DOMAINS
1. Governance
2. Scope
3. Schedule
4. Finance
5. Stakeholders
6. Resources
7. Risk

Important: Quality is not one of these seven performance-domain headings. Quality remains a major project-management concern and is explicit in ECO Process Task 7 and PMBOK 8 quality content.

## 6. PMBOK 8 DOMAIN MASTERY MAP

### Governance
Value creation, governance models, metrics/mechanisms, decision structures, authority/accountability, organizational alignment, governance interactions, verifying results.
Question: Who has authority, what rules apply, how are decisions made/escalated, and how is success verified?

### Scope
Product/project scope, requirements, decomposition, WBS, validation, control, tailoring, interactions with schedule/finance/stakeholders/resources/risk.
Distinguish product scope, project scope, requirements, requested change and approved change.

### Schedule
Activities, sequencing, effort/duration, dependencies, schedule adjustment, critical path, baseline, monitoring, adaptive scheduling, historical/benchmark data.
PMBOK 8 stresses contextual adaptation of scheduling methods.

### Finance
Cost, financing, budgeting, funding, reserves, financial reporting, forecasting, cost measurement/control, value and ROI/long-term impact.
PMBOK 8 emphasizes using financial data for decisions, not merely collecting it.
Key EVM concepts include CV, SV, VAC, CPI, SPI and TCPI; exact formulas must be implemented only from verified source context.

### Stakeholders
Identification, analysis, engagement, communication linkages, negotiation, conflict management, stakeholder influence on decisions.
Critical distinction:
Communication = information exchange.
Engagement = participation/alignment/relationship needed to accomplish objectives.
ECO maps these to **separate tasks** T4 (engagement) and T8 (communication).

### Resources
Human + physical/virtual resources; availability, acquisition, utilization, maintenance, team effectiveness, motivation, empowerment, skills, professional/ethical behavior, organizational/cultural/geographic context.

### Risk
Uncertainty, anticipation, preparation, response, adaptation, resilience, identification, analysis, response, monitoring/control.
Distinguish:
- Risk = uncertain event/condition.
- Issue = current condition/problem.
- Impediment/blocker = obstacle preventing progress.
A risk may become an issue when it materializes.

## 7. DELIVERY APPROACHES
PMBOK 8 recognizes predictive, adaptive, agile, iterative, incremental, hybrid and change-oriented approaches.
Approach selection is contextual.
Factors include innovation, requirement certainty, scope stability, ease/cost of change, delivery options, project factors and organizational factors.
Do not convert these into absolute rules.
Hybrid may range from predictive-dominant to balanced to adaptive-dominant.

## 8. QUALITY
Quality should be integrated into processes and deliverables, not treated only as final inspection.
Lesson 5 covers quality requirements, quality planning/tools, quality management plan, regulatory compliance, cost of quality, sustainability, continuous reviews and continuous improvement.
Cost of Quality categories taught in Lesson 5:
- prevention
- appraisal
- internal failure
- external failure

## 9. VALUE / OUTCOMES
Current PMP exam and PMBOK 8 emphasize value, outcomes, stakeholder value, business impact and continuing business value.
Do not reduce success to scope + schedule + cost.
Decision analysis should consider outcome, stakeholder needs, value, feasibility, risk, sustainability, finance and governance.

## 10. LEADERSHIP
PMBOK 8/Lesson 7 emphasize accountable leadership, integrity, respect, adaptability, shared leadership, psychological safety, influence, communication, decision making, emotional intelligence and problem solving.
Leadership differs from authority:
authority comes from position; leadership involves influencing/inspiring/motivating.
Leadership can be exercised by different project professionals.

Lesson 7 reinforces:
- empathy
- influence
- coaching/mentoring
- active listening
- conflict management
- teamwork
- inspirational leadership
- leadership-style selection
- role clarity
- empowerment

## 11. LESSON 2 KNOWLEDGE
ECO: 1.1, 1.4, 1.5 and 3.1.
Stakeholder analysis techniques in the lesson include questionnaires/surveys, brainstorming, document analysis, stakeholder analysis, power/interest, power/influence, impact/influence, salience model and influence directions.
Expectation-alignment meetings: prepare relevant information, define objectives, facilitate discussion, align expectations and establish actions.
Governance alignment: understand governance structure, provide appropriate information to governance bodies, follow milestone/phase-gate review processes.

## 12. LESSON 5 KNOWLEDGE
ECO: 2.5 procurement, 2.7 quality, 2.8 schedule.
Procurement: planning, contract selection, vendor performance, negotiation, negotiation strategy, supplier/contract management, procurement strategy.
Contract considerations: responsibility, warranties, waivers, termination, contingency; collaborate with legal teams.
Schedule management plan may include schedule model, maintenance/update, accuracy ranges, measurement units, organizational links, control thresholds, performance rules, reporting and process descriptions.
Adaptive estimation examples: relative sizing, story points, planning poker, historical/benchmark data.

## 13. LESSON 7 KNOWLEDGE
ECO: 1.2 conflict, 1.3 team leadership.
Conflict work: identify source, analyze context, active listening, empathy, influence, leadership and decision making.
Approaches taught:
- accommodate
- avoid/withdraw
- compromise/reconcile
- force/direct
- collaborate/problem solve
The best response depends on context; do not memorize one universal approach.
Team work: expectations, empowerment, problem solving, team voice, varied perspectives, leadership style, roles/responsibilities.
Emotional intelligence: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness/empathy, relationship management, influence, coaching/mentoring, conflict management, teamwork, inspirational leadership.

## 15. MASTER QUESTION DESIGN
Questions must test judgment, not keyword recognition.
Prefer realistic scenarios, FIRST/NEXT/BEST questions, plausible distractors, context-dependent decisions, cross-domain reasoning and predictive/adaptive/hybrid variation.
Avoid trivia, keyword giveaways, absolute rules, repeated templates and unsupported PMI claims.

Every future question should carry:
ECO domain/task; PMBOK performance domain where relevant; concept; sub-concept; approach; difficulty; cognitive demand; answer/rationale; distractor rationale; misconception; source reference; language; duplicate fingerprint.
